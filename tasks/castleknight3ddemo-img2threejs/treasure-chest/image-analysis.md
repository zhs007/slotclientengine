# Treasure chest image analysis

## Identification

- Primary type: closed medieval treasure chest; primary domain: object; confidence 0.98.
- Reference contains four consistent views of one stylized prop on a neutral background.
- Intended use: real-time browser slot symbol, clickable and able to expose a lid pivot.

## Overall form and silhouette

- Bilaterally symmetric compound hard-surface object inside a squat cuboid volume.
- Width is approximately 1.45 times height; depth is approximately 0.75 times width.
- Lower body is a beveled rectangular chest; lid is a half-cylinder shell with a strongly arched profile.
- Silhouette is interrupted by two thick lid hoops, projecting corner guards, a front gem escutcheon and side hinge plates.

## Macro, meso and micro hierarchy

- Macro: lower wooden body; arched lid; front lock assembly; metal reinforcement cage.
- Meso: horizontal wood plank courses; two gold lid hoops; four vertical corner posts; top/bottom rails; rear hinge straps; side latch plates.
- Micro: round rivets; bevel bands; wood-grain relief; hammered-metal value patches; seam grooves; localized edge chips.

## Spatial relationships

- The lid overlaps the body at the upper seam and rotates around two rear hinge sockets.
- Gold rails overlap the wooden shell; vertical posts butt into the top and bottom rails.
- The purple gem is embedded in a layered pointed escutcheon, centered on the forward face.
- Rivets are embedded in the outward-facing reinforcement plates rather than floating above them.

## Materials and finish

- Wood: opaque dielectric, warm brown albedo, roughness about 0.68, shallow directional grain and darker recessed plank seams.
- Gold: metallic, warm yellow-orange albedo, roughness about 0.3, faceted bevel highlights and subtle hammered variation.
- Iron: metallic, dark neutral violet-gray albedo, roughness about 0.42, localized brighter rivet caps.
- Gem: opaque-to-semitranslucent stylized crystal, saturated violet albedo, low roughness and emissive center accents.

## Identity-defining features

- Strong semicircular lid with two oversized gold hoops.
- Large pointed double-layer gold escutcheon and faceted purple gem.
- Chunky gold corner blocks with visible rivets.
- Rear paired iron hinge straps and coherent reinforcement cage.
- Three-to-four readable wood plank courses with sculpted, uneven grain.

## Uncertainty

- Exact underside construction is hidden and will use a simple inset base.
- Interior is not visible; closed-state fidelity is authoritative. The lid pivot will still be action-ready.
- Small wear marks vary between generated views; they will be represented as bounded procedural texture rather than copied one-for-one.

## Suitability verdict

Pass. The target is isolated, materials and four silhouettes are visible, hidden regions are inferable, and all major forms are procedural-primitives compatible. Reconstruction remains a stylized approximation rather than exact mesh extraction.
